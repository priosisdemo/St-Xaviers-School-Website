Welcome to St. Xavier's

Founded in 2012, St. Xavier's Senior Secondary School is a CBSE-affiliated, co-educational, English-medium school offering education from Nursery to Class 12, with Science, Commerce and Arts streams at the senior secondary level. We believe every child deserves a caring environment, dedicated teachers and opportunities to discover their potential.
